Questions

Before you book.

Who is the intro call for?

Founders and owners of startups and small businesses, and companies with a product that needs market access in Canada or the United States, especially from the Middle East and North Africa, South America and South Asia. It also suits Canadian businesses buying, selling or restructuring.

Do I need to be in Canada?

No. Intro calls are online, by video, and I work across time zones from Vancouver to the Gulf.

What do I get from 90 minutes?

A clear picture of where your business stands, honest general advice on your biggest questions, and practical next steps. If you need a full analysis or plan, I’ll explain what that would involve and what it would cost.

Why is the intro call paid?

So the time is fully yours. It’s a working session, not a sales pitch, and you get my full attention on your business.

How much do larger projects cost?

It depends on the team and scope. A project might need a financial analyst, a lawyer, an HR professional or a software engineer. After we understand what you need, you get a specific cost before any work starts.

Is this legal or financial advice?

The intro call is general business advice. When a project needs formal legal, accounting or HR advice, we bring in qualified professionals.
