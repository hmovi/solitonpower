How Soliton Power works.

A portfolio,
run as one.

Soliton Power owns and operates companies in trade, training, marketing, consulting, software, and events and travel. Each keeps its own brand and clients, and all share one management team.

Power management

We run the operations that let companies grow, so founders and teams can focus on their work.

Local where it counts

Vancouver, Whistler, Squamish and the lower mainland, with reach into Africa, Asia and South America through trade.

Shared systems, specialist companies

Each company is a specialist in its field. Shared operations and support sit behind all of them.

Looking for a service?

Each company in the portfolio works with its own clients. Start with the company that fits, or contact Soliton Power if you’re not sure where to begin.
