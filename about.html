Soliton Power is a Vancouver group that runs companies in trade, training, marketing, management consulting, software, and events and travel. Each serves its own clients under its own brand. We provide the leadership and operations behind them.

We’re cowboy entrepreneurs. We’re the support, strategy, capital and expertise behind scaling successful ventures, and we back our people to fire on all cylinders.

Our companies serve clients under their own brands, so for trade, training, marketing, management, software, or events and travel, start with the one that fits.
