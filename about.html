Soliton Power is based in Vancouver, Canada. We run a portfolio of companies and provide the power management and operations team behind their success.

Who we are.

Soliton Power is a Vancouver portfolio of companies: Soliton Trade Advising, CTLS, Red Chair Marketing, Soliton Management Services and Tectostack.

The companies cover trade advising, training and learning systems, digital marketing, operations and management services, and software development. Each one serves its own clients under its own brand. Soliton Power runs the portfolio and the management and operations that sit behind it.
