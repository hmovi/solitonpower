Who we are.

Soliton Power is a Vancouver portfolio of companies: 49 West Trade Consulting, CTLS, Red Chair Marketing and Soliton Small Business Consulting.

The companies cover trade advising, training and learning systems, digital marketing and small business consulting. Each one serves its own clients under its own brand. Soliton Power provides the management and operations that sit behind them, through Soliton Management Services.
