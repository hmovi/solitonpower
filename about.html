One group. Five specialist companies.

Soliton Power is a Vancouver group that runs companies in trade, training, marketing, management consulting and software. Each serves its own clients under its own brand. We provide the leadership and operations behind them.

What Soliton Power is

We’re the umbrella over our companies. We set direction, share operational know-how and keep the businesses running well, so each team can focus on the clients it serves.

We don’t offer services directly. If you need help with trade, training, marketing, management or software, start with the company that fits.

Specialist companies

Each company does one thing well, for the clients who need it.

Shared foundation

Operations, management and planning sit at the group level, so no company builds them from scratch.

Rooted in Vancouver

Local knowledge, with reach into Africa, Asia and South America through our trade work.
