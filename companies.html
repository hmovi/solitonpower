International trade

49 WestTrade Consulting

49 West Trade Consulting advises companies in Africa, Asia and South America that want to import into, or expand in, the United States and Canada.

From understanding the market to planning your entry, the team helps overseas businesses approach North America with a clear plan.

  • Import advising for the US and Canada
  • Market entry and expansion planning
  • Support for companies in Africa, Asia and South America

Who it serves: Exporters and growing businesses abroad that want a foothold in North America.

Training and learning

CTLSCanada Training and Learning Systems

Canada Training and Learning Systems (CTLS) builds training and learning systems for colleges and organizations, using Canadian professionals.

CTLS brings subject experts and learning design together, so programs are practical, well structured and ready to deliver.

  • Learning systems for colleges
  • Training programs for organizations
  • Content designed and delivered by Canadian professionals

Who it serves: Colleges, training providers and organizations that need structured learning.

Digital marketing

  • Website development
  • Content creation: copy, photos and video
  • Social media management
  • E-commerce management

Who it serves: Local businesses, from gyms and cafés to growing companies, in Vancouver, Whistler and Squamish.
